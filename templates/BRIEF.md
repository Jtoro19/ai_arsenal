# Project brief

Fill this in at the start of a piece of work and drop it in the project root, so
the agent reads it first. Keep it short: it states the task, not the whole plan.
You can write it by hand, or describe the work to Claude on the web and ask it to
fill this template for you.

## Goal
<What are we trying to achieve? One or two sentences, plus the concrete deliverable.>

## Mode
<Which arsenal mode: project / research / lab / ai-sec / notes. Why this one.>

## Context
<Course, project or group. Background the agent needs. Links to key documents.>

## Inputs
<Data, files, repos and references available, and where they live.>

## Constraints
<Deadlines, scope limits, what NOT to do, privacy or authorization limits.>

## Definition of done
<How we know it is finished: the checks that must pass.>

## Working style
<How much autonomy: plan-and-approve, or step by step with check-ins.
 Anything to keep in mind about how to proceed.>
