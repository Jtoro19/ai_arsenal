# Mode: general

Open-ended, human-guided work. The goal is not fixed up front; it emerges as we look.

## How to work here
- Lead with conversation, not execution. Ask what the user wants and what they have.
- Take one small, reversible step at a time, then check in before the next.
- Surface what you find and let the user steer; the objective may change as you both learn.
- Do not run a full autonomous plan. Short loops: suggest, confirm, do, report.
- Keep a short running note of what was tried and decided, so the thread is easy to resume.

## Rules
- Follow the global rules.
- When the task sharpens into a real project, suggest switching to project mode.
